Let's Hush.

Hold a key, speak naturally, and release. Your thin desktop client streams audio to Hush Cloud and inserts the finished text wherever your cursor is.

Permissions

Hush requests microphone, hotkey, and typing access as you enable them.

🎙
Microphone
Required to hear your voice
⏳
📋 What to do
  1. Choose Allow in the macOS microphone dialog.
  2. Come back here. This check updates automatically.
⌨
Input Monitoring
Required to detect your global hotkey
⏳
What to do in System Settings
  1. Turn on Hush in Input Monitoring.
  2. If Hush is absent, use + and select /Applications/Hush.app.
  3. Restart Hush if macOS asks, then return to this page.
♿
Accessibility
Required to type into other apps
⏳
📋 What to do in System Settings
  1. Choose Open System Settings in the macOS alert.
  2. Turn on Hush in Accessibility.
  3. Come back here. The ❌ will turn ✅ automatically.
