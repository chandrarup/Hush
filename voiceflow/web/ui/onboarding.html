Welcome to Hush

Hold a key, speak naturally, and release. Polished text appears wherever your cursor is.

100% local. No cloud. No subscriptions.

Permissions

Hush needs three permissions. Click Open Settings and follow the steps shown.

🎙
Microphone
Required to hear your voice
⏳
📋 What to do in System Settings
  1. System Settings opens to Privacy & Security → Microphone
  2. Find Terminal (or Python) in the list
  3. Toggle it ON
  4. Come back here. The ❌ will turn ✅ automatically.
⌨️
Input Monitoring
Required to detect your hotkey globally
⏳
📋 What to do in System Settings
  1. System Settings opens to Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring
  2. Click + if Terminal/Python is not listed, then add it
  3. Toggle it ON
⚠️ Input Monitoring needs a restart to take effect. Hush will restart automatically after setup.
♿
Accessibility
Required to type into other apps
⏳
📋 What to do in System Settings
  1. System Settings opens to Privacy & Security → Accessibility
  2. Click the + button if Terminal/Python is not listed
  3. Find and add Terminal (or your Python app)
  4. Toggle it ON
  5. Come back here. The ❌ will turn ✅ automatically.
